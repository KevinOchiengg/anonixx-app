# Gulf workers drafts: 9 official drops (2026-10-29)

All items are `status: draft`. Nothing has been posted. The founder approves or rejects each one (TEAM.md rule 3).

## Notes for the founder

- **Voice change (TEAM.md rule 2).** The brief asked for first-person confessions "the way a real worker would write". Official drops show as "Anonixx", and posting an invented first-person worker story under that name, or teasing it on Facebook, would read as a fake real confession. These drafts are written in Anonixx's own voice, speaking to the worker in second person. They stay raw and specific and end with an ask. If you want first-person versions, they need to be clearly framed as Anonixx prompts and not as user stories.
- **Neutral (rule 5a).** The drop text names no country, city, employer, nationality, religion or currency. "Over here" and "back home" do the work. Hashtags are generic too: #OFW and #KSA were left out.
- **Timing.** Gulf evenings (9pm to 1am, UTC+3/+4) are about 18:00 to 22:00 UTC. Friday is the common day off, so Thursday and Friday nights are probably strongest. This is a guess and has not been measured.
- **Avoided on purpose.** There is 2026 news coverage of strikes hitting the Gulf and the fear this causes workers' families (Al Jazeera, Global Voices). It is too raw and too close to exploitative for a hook, so none of the drafts use it.
- **Moderation.** Drafts 3 and 4 may draw replies about abuse, confiscated documents or self-harm. Have moderation and a help or safety resource ready before approving them.

## Research: what could and couldn't be accessed

- **Facebook groups: could not access.** Group search and group pages need a login (the fetch returned a 404 or login wall). No Facebook posts or comments were read. Nothing below claims to come from them.
- **No Reddit, TikTok or YouTube comment threads were reached directly.** Comment-theme evidence comes from news and research that describe them.
- **Public sources used:**
  - Netnography of a Gulf migrant-worker Facebook community: members use it for information, emotional support and stories of mistreatment ([LSPR Communicare 2023](https://journal.lspr.ac.id/index.php/communicare/article/download/621/258/2129)).
  - Domestic workers' venting videos reach hundreds of thousands of views, at personal risk ([Fuller Project](https://archive.fullerproject.org/impact-stories/domestic-workers-in-gulf-countries-use-tiktok-to-vent-their-woes-despite-risks/)).
  - Amnesty's 2025 report on 70+ women: 16-hour days, no days off, passports and phones confiscated ([Amnesty](https://www.amnesty.org/en/documents/mde23/9222/2025/en/), [Nation](https://nation.africa/kenya/news/-kafala-the-silent-slavery-behind-torture-abuse-of-kenyans-in-saudi-arabia-5041352)).
  - Viral reunion and farewell videos: one airport goodbye had 8.6M views and 200k+ shares, and commenters said they had missed big family moments too ([GMA](https://gmanetwork.com/news/pinoyabroad/dispatch/588954/emotional-parting-of-young-girl-ofw-father-in-davao-airport/story), [GMA Qatar surprise](https://gmanetwork.com/news/pinoyabroad/dispatch/652099/ofw-in-qatar-disguises-as-a-waiter-to-surprise-his-kids-in-phl/story), [GMA missed milestones](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/pinoyabroad/dispatch/988474/filipino-mother-ofw-jordan-sacrifice/story/)).
  - Family assumes endless money; returnees swarmed with requests ([Palawan Daily News](https://palawandailynews.com/column/how-common-money-mistakes-prevent-ofws-from-coming-home-for-good/), [LaSallian](https://thelasallian.com/2025/04/03/packages-of-love-unwrapping-the-balikbayan-box/)).
  - Left-behind spouses, marriage strain and stigma ([NHRC Nepal study](https://elibrary.nhrc.gov.np/bitstream/20.500.14356/2984/1/2.Anup%20Adhikari_THE%20CONJUGAL%20LIFE%20OF%20INTERNATIONAL%20MIGRANTS%27%20LEFT-BEHIND%20SPOUSES%20IN%20EASTERN%20NEPAL.pdf), [Onlinekhabar](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/foreign-employment-nepal-families.html), [Himal](https://himalmag.com/nepali-migrant-workers-wives-sexuality-policing/), [The Quint: 70% of left-behind wives report anxiety and loneliness](https://thequint.com/news/india/wives-of-migrant-workers-left-behind-unaware-of-gulf-crisis-tamil-nadu)).
  - Isolation and calls home as a lifeline ([Georgetown CIRS](https://cirs.qatar.georgetown.edu/overcoming-isolation-migrants-connectivity-and-covid-19-qatar/), [Coda Story](https://www.codastory.com/?p=12637)).
  - Unfinished houses and money that didn't build the dream ([The East African](https://theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/news/world/qatar-migrant-workers-dreams-turn-to-dust-4022284), [Eco-Business](https://www.eco-business.com/zh-hans/news/once-heroes-indias-ex-gulf-workers-forge-new-futures/), [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/as-cash-from-gulf-dries-up-families-fret/)).
  - Reverse culture shock on return ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2021/12/30/my-reverse-culture-shock-returning-to-the-uk-after-a-decade-in-dubai)).
- **Gaps.** No engagement numbers for specific Facebook pages. The "what drives comments" claims are inferred from viral-video coverage and research, not measured.

### 1. The milestone you watched on a screen
status: draft
type: prompt
card_style: Skeleton In The Closet
suggested_time: 2026-10-30 18:30 (UTC)
theme: Missing children's milestones.

teaser: Some of us watched our kids grow up through a phone. Say the moment out loud, anonymously, on Anonixx. #WorkingAbroad #MissingHome #ParentsAbroad
