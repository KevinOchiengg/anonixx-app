# Anonixx sound shortlist (drafts for the admin Sounds page)

Compiled 2026-10-24 by the content scout. Nothing has been uploaded. The founder decides.

## What's in here
- **30 tracks, all CC0 (Creative Commons 0 / public-domain dedication).** I checked the license on each track's own Freesound page on 2026-10-24. CC0 means no attribution is needed, commercial use is fine, and you can edit and redistribute. Nothing here is NC, ND, Pixabay-only or unclear, so no Pixabay or CC BY tracks were needed.
- No commercial songs. Some uploaders tag their beats with artist names ("Drake type", "Chris Brown type"). That's just producer slang for a style. The audio is their own work. Don't use those names anywhere in the app.
- Moods use the exact backend values: `any`, `longing`, `untold`, `horny`, `discreet`.

## Before uploading (backend limit: mp3/m4a only, 5 MB max)
- **Download:** every source is Freesound. Each page has a preview player. The full download needs a free Freesound login (Download button on the page). I couldn't confirm any public direct-download URLs, so the source page is the link in every entry.
- **Converting:** many originals are WAV/FLAC or over 5 MB. Export them as 128 kbps mp3. At that bitrate 5 MB is about 5 min 20 s, so anything longer must be trimmed. Each entry says what it needs.
- **Kontraa flag (5 tracks, marked [K]):** `kontraamusic` is a commercial music brand ("Kontraa Studio / UKA Music Publishing LLC"). Its profile lists a publisher IPI and a DDEX ID. It uploaded these tracks itself as CC0, so using them in the app is clean. The risk: tracks registered with a publisher can trigger YouTube/TikTok Content ID claims if a user screen-records a drop and reposts it. That's a small risk and not a license problem. It's still worth knowing before using them in our own TikTok ads.
- **Courtesy credit (optional):** AudioWay asks for credit as "The Audio Way" or "Kevin Brown" *if required*. CC0 doesn't require it.
