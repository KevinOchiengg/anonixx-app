## Technical fixes (ranked by impact)

1. **The share domain doesn't exist. Every shared link and referral is dead.** Production serves `anonixx.com` (`Web/middleware.ts:10` `CANONICAL_HOST = "anonixx.com"`, and the live sitemap). But `anonixx.app` doesn't resolve, and the code hard-codes it in:
   - `Backend/app/api/v1/whatsapp.py:38` `SHARE_BASE_URL = "https://anonixx.app/drop"`
   - `Backend/app/api/v1/referrals.py:76,222` `https://anonixx.app/join?ref=…`
   - `Backend/app/services/caption_engine.py:48-78` (every TikTok and social caption CTA)
   - `Frontend/src/navigation/AppNavigator.jsx:67-68` (deep-link prefixes)
   - Emails and contact addresses: `Backend/app/config.py:87,91`, `Web/app/privacy/page.tsx:16,18`, `Web/app/delete-account/page.tsx:12`, `Frontend/src/screens/settings/LegalScreen.jsx:96`

   That means zero backlinks or referral signups from shares, and the privacy@ and noreply@ addresses probably bounce. **Change:** choose one domain. The fastest fix is anonixx.com, which is already live and canonical. Replace `anonixx.app` with `anonixx.com` in those URLs (add `https://anonixx.com` and `https://www.anonixx.com` to `AppNavigator.jsx` prefixes and Android App Links). If the founder owns anonixx.app, also point its DNS at Vercel and add it to `LEGACY_HOSTS` (`Web/middleware.ts:9`) so it 308-redirects to anonixx.com. Fix the email domain separately (MX records, or move to @anonixx.com). This is also the domain mismatch the product manager flagged. Nothing else in this report matters as much.

2. **There's no default share image, so home and `/join` previews are bare text.** There's no `opengraph-image` or `twitter-image` in `Web/app/` and no image in `openGraph` (`Web/app/layout.tsx:28-29`). Text-only drops get none either (`Web/app/drop/[id]/page.tsx:22,35`). **Change:** add `Web/app/opengraph-image.tsx` (Next `ImageResponse`, 1200x630, the brand line "Say what you can't say out loud." on the dark theme). For text drops, add `Web/app/drop/[id]/opengraph-image.tsx` that renders the teaser as a card in the brand serif. Only for `general` drops; restricted drops already return early.

3. **`SITE_URL` silently falls back to localhost.** `Web/lib/api.ts:4` defaults to `http://localhost:3000`. If `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` is ever missing in a deploy, canonicals, `metadataBase`, the sitemap and robots all point to localhost. It's correct in production today. **Change:** default to `"https://anonixx.com"` instead of localhost, or throw at build time when `VERCEL_ENV === "production"` and the variable is unset.

4. **No canonical on home, `/join`, `/terms`, `/privacy`.** Only drop pages set one (`Web/app/drop/[id]/page.tsx:28`). Tracking parameters like `?ref=` on `/join` (from `referrals.py:76`) create duplicate URLs. **Change:** add `alternates: { canonical: "/" }` in `Web/app/layout.tsx:19-31` (it resolves against `metadataBase`), and `alternates: { canonical: "/join" }` in `Web/app/join/page.tsx:4-7`.

5. **`/join` is too thin to rank for "anonymous confession app".** `Web/app/join/page.tsx:12-24` is an h1 and one sentence. **Change:** turn it into the real app landing page. Cover what Anonixx is, how anonymity works, that link up only happens when both people say yes, 18+ rules, safety and moderation, a short FAQ with `FAQPage` JSON-LD, and a Play link. Title: "Anonixx: the anonymous confession app". Keep the voice short and on-brand.

6. **Drop pages have no h1, and every page repeats the same 10 feed posts.** `Web/app/drop/[id]/page.tsx:71` renders `<Feed>` with the drop plus `fetchPublicFeed()`. The h1 only exists in `Hero` (`Web/components/Hero.tsx:7`), which drop pages don't show. Each drop page's HTML is mostly the same feed, which Google reads as thin or duplicate content. **Change:** render the drop text as a visually hidden or styled `<h1>` (teaser) before `<Feed>`. Server-render only the target drop and let the following posts load on the client, or mark the following slides with `data-nosnippet`.

7. **Very short drops get indexed as thin pages.** The sitemap lists every general drop (`Backend/app/api/v1/public.py:68-82`, `Web/app/sitemap.ts:23-31`). **Change:** list only drops with content of at least about 80 characters, or with some engagement (`likes_count + thread_count` of at least 1), and set `robots: { index: false, follow: true }` on the rest in `generateMetadata` (`Web/app/drop/[id]/page.tsx:20`). This keeps crawl budget for pages that can rank.

8. **Structured data gaps.** The home page JSON-LD (`Web/app/page.tsx:9-15`) has no `logo`, `sameAs` or `description`. Drop JSON-LD (`Web/app/drop/[id]/page.tsx:59`) uses `Person` for official drops. **Change:** add `logo: SITE_URL + "/icon.png"`, `description`, and `sameAs` (Play Store and social profiles) to Organization. Add `potentialAction` only if a public search exists (it doesn't, since `/search` is disallowed). For `post.is_admin_drop`, set `author` to the Anonixx Organization.

9. **Drop titles can go generic.** `Web/app/drop/[id]/page.tsx:23` sets the title to just the quoted teaser, and image-only drops get `“”`. **Change:** `const title = teaser(post.content, 60) ? \`“${teaser(post.content, 60)}”\` : "An anonymous confession"`. The template adds "| Anonixx".

10. **Small items.**
    - Hookup posts are correctly kept out of search. They're forced to `mature` (`Backend/app/api/v1/drops.py:854`), excluded from the sitemap (`public.py:70`) and set to noindex (`drop/[id]/page.tsx:16-18`). No change needed.
    - `/login`, `/signup` and `/room/*` are already noindex. Good.
    - Page speed: feed images use `next/image` with `priority` only on nearby slides (`Web/components/Slide.tsx:302,320`, `FitImage.tsx:17-18`). That's fine. The AdSense script (`layout.tsx:49-56`) loads `afterInteractive` on every public page, including drop landing pages. Watch INP and CLS once Search Console is connected.
    - The share locale is already `en_US` (`Web/app/layout.tsx:28`), per the product manager's change. Done.

## 3 page briefs

All three are original writing. Example confessions are **official Anonixx drops or public `general` drops only**, never mature or hookup posts, and never anything from Reddit or TikTok. No place names.

### Brief 1. Topic hub: situationship confessions
- **Title:** Situationship confessions: what people never said out loud | Anonixx
- **Slug:** `/confessions/situationship`
- **Target keyword:** situationship confessions (secondary: secret confessions, anonymous confessions)
- **Build note:** posts only have `mood_tag` (longing, untold, horny, discreet; default longing, `Backend/app/api/v1/drops.py:1730`), which isn't topical. This needs an admin-set `topic` field, or a curated list of official and public drop IDs. **Only launch a hub once it has 15 or more real drops**, otherwise it's a doorway page. Use the same template later for `/confessions/late-night` (keyword #8), and keep the number of hubs small.
- **Outline:**
  1. H1 and a 2-sentence original intro: what a situationship is, and why people only admit it anonymously.
  2. A live grid of 15 to 30 `general` drops on the topic, server-rendered, each linking to `/drop/<id>`.
  3. "The things nobody says": 3 short original paragraphs on recurring themes (never knowing what you are, not being allowed to be jealous, the slow fade). Themes only, no quotes.
  4. FAQ (2 to 3 questions, `FAQPage` JSON-LD): "Is it a situationship or a relationship?", "Should I confess how I feel?"
  5. Links to related hubs and the confess guide.
- **CTA:** "Say yours. Nobody will know it was you." Button: Drop a confession, which goes to `/signup?next=/compose`.

### Brief 2. Guide: how to confess anonymously
- **Title:** How to confess anonymously (and actually stay anonymous)
- **Slug:** `/guides/how-to-confess-anonymously`
- **Target keyword:** confess anonymously (secondary: anonymous confession app, confession website)
- **Outline:**
  1. Short intro: why people confess anonymously (relief, testing a feeling, saying it to someone without the fallout).
  2. Ways to do it, honestly compared: a notes app, an anonymous Q&A link, a confession app. Cover what each one hides and what it leaks.
  3. Staying anonymous: leave out names, workplaces and identifying details; strip photo metadata; don't post from shared devices; know what a platform can see.
  4. How it works on Anonixx: posting is free, your name stays hidden, and link up only happens if both say yes (for hookup posts, 18+ only). Factual, no hype.
  5. When a confession is really a crisis: point to findahelpline.com (global). This matches the product manager's region-aware helpline plan.
  6. FAQ (`FAQPage` JSON-LD).
- **CTA:** "Write it at 3am. Post it hidden." Button: Confess on Anonixx, which goes to `/signup?next=/compose`.

### Brief 3. Guide: meeting someone from an app safely (18+, non-explicit)
- **Title:** How to meet someone from an app safely
- **Slug:** `/guides/meet-someone-safely`
- **Target keyword:** how to meet someone from an app safely (secondary, used sparingly: anonymous hookup app)
- **Tone and limits:** clearly 18+ (a label at the top), no explicit language or images, no keyword stuffing. It's a safety page first. This is the page that can win trust for the hookup side without fighting the affiliate sites head-on.
- **Outline:**
  1. "18+ only" note, then the intro: anonymity is the point until you both decide it isn't.
  2. Before you meet: chat first, video-check, never send money, keep your number and socials private until you're ready.
  3. Picking the place: public, your own way there and back, tell a friend, share your live location with someone you trust.
  4. On the day: trust your gut, leave anytime, consent is ongoing.
  5. After: block and report tools; what to do if something goes wrong. Link to the in-app report flow and findahelpline.com.
  6. How Anonixx handles it: hookup posts are 18+ and age-confirmed, link up needs the poster to accept, names stay hidden until then, moderation and reporting.
- **CTA:** "Stay hidden until you both say yes." Button: Join Anonixx (18+), which goes to `/signup`.
