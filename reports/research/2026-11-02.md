# Researcher report, 2026-11-02

Data note: search coverage is thin again. Many items are aggregator headlines or secondary reporting, and several figures have no traceable original study. Treat as background, not confirmed "this week" news.

## Top 3 takeaways
1. Age verification is spreading fast (EU, US app stores, Turkey). The 18+ hookup posts need a privacy-preserving age-check plan.
2. Dating-app fatigue is real: use is falling, so a different, low-pressure format has room.
3. Situationship evidence is mixed. Use it as a theme for drops, not as a hard number.

## Risks
- EU: Commission recommendation (29 Apr 2026) favours anonymous proof-of-age; Member States should offer such services by 31 Dec 2026. [SCL](https://www.scl.org/european-commission-sets-out-approach-for-age-verification/), [Didit guide](https://updates.didit.me/blog/age-verification-laws-2026/)
- US: California AB 1043 (app-store age signals) starts 1 Jan 2027. Alabama signed a similar law (Feb 2026). Texas's was halted by a federal judge. [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/states-of-play/california-enacts-google-backed-law-for-app-stores-to-verify-age), [Didit guide](https://updates.didit.me/blog/age-verification-laws-2026/)
- Turkey: age-verification rules for social networks, domestic and foreign, take effect 1 Nov 2026. [Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/21949-age-verification-requirement-in-regulation-on-provision-of-differentiated-services-for-children-by-social-network-providers-and-age-verification-official-gazette-no-33396)
- Age checks collide with anonymity; storing IDs or selfies adds breach risk (see Tea). [Tuta](https://tuta.com/it/blog/age-verification-kills-anonymity)
