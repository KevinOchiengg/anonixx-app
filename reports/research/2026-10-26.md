# Researcher report, 2026-10-26

Data note: search coverage for this week was thin. Most sources are 2025 or undated, and nothing below is confirmed as "this week". Treat as background.

## Top 3 takeaways
1. Gen Z is pulling back from dating but wants clarity when it does engage: an opening for low-pressure, anonymous expression.
2. Age verification is becoming the default expectation for dating apps (UK Ofcom rules), so the 18+ hookup posts need a credible age-gate plan.
3. Anonymous-message apps carry a deceptive-practice reputation (fake messages, paid "hints"): honesty about what is real is a differentiator.

## Themes this week
- Situationships are common: 60% of US adults report having been in one (Top10.com survey, unverified date). [Source](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/study-60-of-americans-have-been-in-a-situationship/)
- Withdrawal and loneliness: Rasmussen poll says 37% of singles under 30 are not interested in dating. [Source](https://www.yahoo.com/news/single-gen-z-romantics-giving-132700269.html)
- Intentionality: a 2026 BLK report says 48% of surveyed Gen Z app users pre-screen matches on faith, politics and money habits early. Single-company survey, narrow sample. [Source](https://www.tovima.com/wsj/fed-up-with-situationships-gen-z-is-ready-to-commit/amp/)
- Money and housing pressure delaying milestones: from analysis pieces, weak evidence. [Source](https://www.flame.edu.in/in-the-media/gen-z-dating-how-emotional-distance-is-a-core-element-of-todays-dating-culture)

## Competitor moves
- Hinge passed Bumble in app-store revenue June to Sept 2025 (Appfigures); Tinder hit a record month in May 2025. [Source](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20251031/2-mainstream-dating-apps-hit-new-all-time-highs-for-the-first-time-in-a-long-time)
- Tinder launched "modes" for double dating and college users (Sept 2025). Hinge positions on conversation quality. [Source](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/hinge-overtakes-bumble-in-revenue-dating-apps-hit-record-highs/)
- NGL was found to send fake messages and charge for sender "hints"; Snapchat restricted anonymous apps after teen-safety lawsuits (2022). A May 2026 parent guide claims new legal bans and FTC action; unverified. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2022/08/04/top-anonymous-social-app-ngl-forced-to-stop-tricking-its-users)
- Tea app (2025) reported a storage-bucket exposure of user IDs and selfies. [Source](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/sites/the-tea-app)
- Whisper, Sendit, Feeld: no current news found (unclear).

## Risks
- UK Online Safety Act: Ofcom treats dating apps as user-to-user services and expects strong age checks; fines up to 10% of global revenue or £18M. Grindr, Tinder and Pure have rolled out biometric or face-estimation checks. [Source](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/ofcom-mandates-stricter-age-verification-for-dating-apps/), [Source](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/grindr-rolls-out-biometric-age-checks-before-uk-safety-law-deadline/)
- US senators pressed Match Group for romance-scam prevention evidence (deadline Oct 15, outcome unclear). [Source](https://www.fastcompany.com/section/tinder)
- Storing ID or selfies creates breach exposure (see Tea). Prefer on-device or third-party estimation with deletion.

## Ideas for Anonixx
1. **Age-gate plan for hookup posts**: evaluate face-estimation vendors that delete data after the check. Evidence: Ofcom expectations, Grindr/Pure rollouts. Owner: product.
2. **"Nothing is fake" trust line**: state in onboarding that every reply and link up is from a real person, and that official drops are labelled "Anonixx". Evidence: NGL fake-message history. Owner: product, content scout.
3. **Drops on situationship and "what are we" themes**, original text, no names or places. Evidence: situationship prevalence. Owner: content scout.
4. **Low-pressure night-time drops for people who have stopped dating** (loneliness angle, no push to link up). Evidence: Rasmussen poll. Owner: content scout.
5. **Optional "clarity" prompts** on hookup posts (what I want, what I don't) to match the prequalifying trend. Evidence: BLK report (narrow sample). Owner: product.
