# Researcher report, 2026-11-23

Data note: search coverage is thin again. Results are mostly aggregator summaries and undated pieces; nothing confirmed as "this week" for Tea, Sendit or Whisper. Primary sources not checked.

## Top 3 takeaways
1. Apple's new age-rating rule is the most concrete item: from 16 Nov 2026, apps with dating or romantic features get a minimum 18+ rating, and developers must answer two new questions in App Store Connect by 12 Jan 2027.
2. Dating-app payer numbers keep falling (Match and Bumble, Q2) while Gen Z moves toward in-person and events. Low-pressure, pay-only-when-it-matters positioning stays open.
3. The anonymous-messaging space is consolidating or fading: NGL was acquired, Whisper reportedly stopped operating. Fewer rivals for anonymous confessions, but a cautionary tale on monetisation tricks.
