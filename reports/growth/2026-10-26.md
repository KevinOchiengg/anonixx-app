## What's new since yesterday
- Researcher report ([2026-10-26](../research/2026-10-26.md)) gives three angles I'm folding into the plan:
  1. **Trust is a differentiator.** NGL was forced to stop faking messages and selling "hints" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/08/04/top-anonymous-social-app-ngl-forced-to-stop-tricking-its-users)). Our line: "Nothing is fake. Every reply is a real person. Drops from us say Anonixx."
  2. **Situationships / "what are we"** is common (60% of US adults report one, unverified date, [source](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/study-60-of-americans-have-been-in-a-situationship/)). Good hook territory, written as original, place-free text.
  3. **Gen Z pulling back from dating** (37% of singles under 30 not interested, Rasmussen via [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/single-gen-z-romantics-giving-132700269.html)). Angle: low-pressure confession, no push to link up.
- Age-check expectations are rising for dating apps (Ofcom, per researcher). Any "18+" ad or creator brief must lead with confessions, not hookups, until the age-gate plan exists.
- Faceless short video research this run was thin and mostly vendor blogs. Only repeated, unverified claim: opening 3 seconds and retention drive reach, stick to one niche for ~30 videos ([Argil](https://argil.ai/blog/faceless-reels-7-methods-to-create-viral-content-without-showing-your-face), [Kineclip](https://kineclip.com/blog/grow-tiktok-without-showing-face/)). One April 2026 piece argues algorithms still favour faces. Treat as hypotheses to test.

## The big bet (2-4 weeks): "Nothing is fake" confession video loop
One niche: late-night unsent-message / situationship confessions, original text, faceless video, 3 posts a day for the first 30 videos. Every video ends "Say yours. anonixx.com/join?ref=<channel>" and every new poster gets a "send it to one person" prompt. Target (a goal, not a forecast, no baseline exists): get a first real baseline for views→click→signup→post within 14 days, and find the 2 hooks that beat the median. Owners: content-scout (drops), tiktok-manager (video), product-manager (referral prompt, `/join`), founder (approvals).
