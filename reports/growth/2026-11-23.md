## Ready-to-approve creative
### 3 short video scripts
1. **"Said it. Nobody knows."** (~8s). 0-2s on-screen: "You said it. Nobody knows it was you." 2-6s: Activity tab, anonymous notification, "But they reacted." 6-8s: "Anonixx. 18+." CTA: link in bio (`?ref=tt-activity`). Alt hooks: 1) The confession got a reply. 2) They never knew who wrote it. 3) You hit send and walked away. 4) Say it once. See what comes back. 5) Anonymous, but not unheard. status: draft
2. **"The sentence you never sent"** (~10s). 0-2s: blank text box, cursor blinking, "The one you never sent." 2-8s: typed line appears word by word, no name, no place; "Send" tapped. 8-10s: "Anonymous. 18+. Anonixx." CTA tag `?ref=tt-neversent`. Alt hooks: 1) Type it. Don't sign it. 2) Everyone has one unsent message. 3) This stays between you and strangers. 4) Write the thing, skip the consequences. 5) Midnight thoughts, no name attached. status: draft
3. **"No reply, coins back"** (~10s). 0-2s: "You can knock without risking much." 2-7s: Knock flow, text overlay "No reply in 7 days, your coins come back." 7-10s: "Anonixx. 18+." Tag `?ref=tt-coinsback`. Alt hooks: 1) Ask first. Pay never, unless it opens. 2) Silence shouldn't cost you. 3) A knock with a safety net. 4) Reach out. We'll hold the coins. 5) Nervous to message? Start here. Check exact wording against the live flow before approving; never claim "pay only if they say yes". status: draft

### Referral / viral-loop upgrade: "Pass it on"
- Mechanic: when a user shares an official drop, the link carries their referral code. If the friend signs up and posts once, both get a small coin credit. Reward amount is the founder's decision (open ask).
- Copy: "Someone should read this. Send it, no name attached."
- Abuse risks: self-referrals with throwaway emails, farming. Mitigate with reward only after first post, per-device cap, delay before coins land.
