## Ready-to-approve creative (status: draft)

### 3 short video scripts
**V1 "No profile"** (~9s). 0-2s on screen: "No profile. No photo. No name." 2-6s: "Just the thing you've never said." 6-9s: "Say it here. 18+." CTA: anonixx.com/join?ref=tt-noprofile.
Alt hooks: 1) Nobody is swiping on you here. 2) Delete the bio. Keep the truth. 3) The only app that doesn't ask what you look like. 4) You don't need a good photo to be honest. 5) Be known for what you say, not how you look.

**V2 "Simmer"** (~10s). 0-2s: "You said 'I don't mind either way.' You minded." 2-7s: "The things we say to stay cool, and the thing we meant." 7-10s: "Say the real one. No name. 18+." CTA: ?ref=tt-simmer.
Alt hooks: 1) You said you were fine. 2) "No pressure" was pressure. 3) The text you sent vs the one you meant. 4) Playing it cool costs something. 5) Who did you pretend not to care about?

**V3 "Read one"** (~10s). 0-2s: "Someone said this tonight." 2-7s: one ORIGINAL official drop as text, no real person's words. 7-10s: "Read more. Say yours. 18+." CTA: ?ref=tt-readone2.
Alt hooks: 1) Tonight's confession. 2) This one stayed with me. 3) No names. Just the truth. 4) What they couldn't say out loud. 5) Anonymous. Still honest.

2. "Honest question from the Anonixx team: what makes an anonymous app feel safe enough to be honest on? We want to know what's missing."

### 1 viral-loop upgrade: "Friend prompt"
- Mechanic: user sends a friend a link to an anonymous prompt ("Something I never told you..."). Friend replies without a name; sender sees it in-app.
- Reward: both get a small coin bonus after the friend's first post (amount: founder decision).
- Copy: "Someone wants to hear what you never said. Reply without your name."
- Abuse risks: link spam, self-referral. Cap sends per day, require the invitee's first post, no fake "someone likes you" alerts, no pre-filled messages.

### 1 guerrilla stunt
Stickers/QR cards: "Not a profile. Say it anyway." Neutral, no place names, 18+ line, QR to `?ref=qr-bio`. Rough cost: small print run, get a quote. Place only where permitted.
