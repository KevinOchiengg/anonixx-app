## Ready-to-approve creative (status: draft)

### 3 short video scripts
**V1 "The unsent one"** (~12s). 0-2s on screen: "The text you wrote and never sent." 2-8s: "We all have one. It's still in your drafts. Here, it can't be traced back to you." 8-12s: "Say it. Nobody will know your name." CTA: anonixx.com/join?ref=tt-unsent (18+).
Alt hooks: 1) Check your drafts. Be honest. 2) Who did you almost text at 1am? 3) Some things only get said to strangers. 4) A secret is lighter when someone else holds it. 5) What are you still carrying?

**V2 "Read one"** (~10s). 0-2s: "Someone said this tonight." 2-7s: show one ORIGINAL official drop as text, no real person's words. 7-10s: "Read more. Say yours. 18+." CTA: ?ref=tt-readone.
Alt hooks: 1) Read what they couldn't say out loud. 2) Tonight's confession. 3) Anonymous. Still honest. 4) This one stayed with me. 5) No names. Just the truth.

**V3 "The question"** (~8s). 0-2s: "What would you say if no one knew it was you?" 2-6s: slow text reveals, three one-line prompts. 6-8s: "Answer it here." CTA: ?ref=tt-question.
Alt hooks: 1) Be honest. Nobody's watching. 2) One thing you'd never say to their face. 3) Your secret, no name. 4) What are you hiding well? 5) Say the thing.

### 5 static / carousel concepts
1. "Things you can't say to their face." Dark card, one line. CTA: Say it here (18+).
2. "No names on confessions." Plain card, trust angle (founder to confirm it matches the product).
3. "Read what others finally said." Three original drops as slides.
4. "Your turn." Cursor blinking on empty text box.

### 3 creator briefs (barter, free coins, no cash)
1. **Faceless storytelling creator.** Angle: read 2 official Anonixx drops, react in their own words. Do: say "#ad / gifted coins", 18+ audience only. Don't: claim it is a hookup guarantee, name real people.
2. **Late-night podcast or relationship page.** Angle: "Would you confess here?" segment. Do: disclose, link `?ref=` tag. Don't: share real users' confessions.
3. **Micro-creator wave (10 small creators).** Angle: own "unsent text" take. Do: original script, disclosure. Don't: fake reactions or invented testimonials.

### 2 community posts (open, as Anonixx; only where rules allow self-promotion)
1. "We're Anonixx, an anonymous confession app (18+). We're not here to post for people. Question for this community: what do you wish you could say anonymously? We'll read replies and write original prompts from the themes, never copying anyone's words."
2. "Honest question from the Anonixx team: what would make an anonymous app feel safe enough to confess on? We're building for that and want to hear what's missing."

### 1 viral-loop upgrade: "Send a confession-prompt"
- Mechanic: user sends a friend a link to an anonymous prompt ("Something I never told you..."). Friend replies anonymously; sender sees it in-app.
- Reward: both get a small coin bonus after the friend's first post (amount: founder decision).
- Copy: "Someone wants to hear what you never said. Reply without your name."
- Abuse risks: link spam, self-referral. Cap sends per day, require the invitee's first post, no deceptive "someone likes you" alerts, no pre-filled fake messages.
