## The big bet (next 2-4 weeks): "Confession-to-share" loop on organic short video
**Idea:** every official drop becomes a faceless 15-25s video whose last frame is "Say yours. anonixx.com/join", and every signup is nudged to send one friend a referral link. Two engines feeding each other: content brings strangers in, referrals compound them.
- **Why now:** zero budget; the loop plumbing (drops, referral coins, OG cards, `/join`) already exists; the SEO keywords we can win are low difficulty (see SEO report), so video plus search is the cheap combo.
- **Target (a goal, not a forecast; no baseline exists):** 3 posts a day across 2 platforms, learn which 3 hooks get the highest view-to-click, and get the referral share rate above 0 in week 1 to have a baseline.
- **Owners:** content-scout (drops), tiktok-manager (video scripts, publisher health), product-manager (funnel fixes), founder (approvals).

## Ready-to-approve creative
Brand voice: short sentences, intimate, no emojis. Location-neutral, 18+ only, no explicit sex.

### 3 short video scripts (`status: draft`)
**V1 "Said it to nobody"** (hook in 2s)
- 0-2s on-screen: "I told no one. So I'm telling you." Voiceover, calm and low.
- 2-12s: one original confession about a text never sent, shown line by line on a dark screen.
- 12-18s: "Some of you have a draft like this."
- CTA: "Say yours. Nobody sees your name. anonixx.com/join"
- Alt hooks: 1) "The text I wrote at 3am and never sent." 2) "Everyone has one message they deleted." 3) "I still think about the thing I didn't say." 4) "Be honest. What's in your drafts?" 5) "This is the lie I tell everyone."

**V2 "Would you want to know?"**
- 0-2s: "Someone nearby wants to meet the person behind this post."
- 2-12s: show a sample official drop, then the Link up request screen (screen recording of the real app only).
- 12-18s: "They only find out if the poster says yes."
- CTA: "Anonymous until you decide. 18+. anonixx.com/join"
- Alt hooks: 1) "You can ask. They can say no. Nobody knows." 2) "The safest way to say 'I like you'." 3) "What if rejection was private?" 4) "You'd never do this out loud. Here you can." 5) "Link up only if you both want to."

**V3 "Vote on a confession"**
- 0-2s: "Is this one wrong?" over a short original dilemma (a friend's secret, a double life, a lie that grew).
- 2-15s: 2 lines of the dilemma, "Vote in the app" text card.
- CTA: "Decide. Then confess your own. anonixx.com/join"
- Alt hooks: 1) "Would you tell her?" 2) "Tell me this isn't a red flag." 3) "Rate this secret." 4) "He asked me not to say. So I'm saying it here." 5) "Judge me. I dare you."

### 5 static / carousel concepts
1. "Say what you can't say out loud." Dark card, one line of text, CTA "Confess anonymously".
2. "3 am confessions" carousel: 5 original lines, last slide "Add yours".
3. "What you didn't send" carousel built on unsent-message themes, original text.
4. "Anonymous until you say yes": 3-slide explainer of Link up.
5. "No names. No profile photos. Just honesty": safety slide (moderation, block, report). Builds trust with new visitors.

### 3 creator briefs (barter, free coins, no cash)
1. **Faceless storyteller (late-night voiceover):** read 1 official drop in your own voice, put your own spin on it. Do: link in bio with your `?ref`, say "partner" or "#ad". Don't: use real people's stories, claim features that don't exist.
2. **Relationship-advice page:** a "what would you do" format using an Anonixx dilemma. Do: disclose, 18+ audience only. Don't: imply the poster is a real named person.
3. **Podcast / newsletter on modern dating:** a short segment on anonymous honesty. Do: honest opinion, disclose the coin gift. Don't: scripted praise, fake testimonials.

### 2 community posts, openly as Anonixx
1. (Where self-promo is allowed) "We're the team behind Anonixx, an anonymous confession app for adults. We'd like your feedback on one thing: what would make you feel safe posting something real? Honest criticism welcome. [link]"
2. "Quick question for anyone who's confessed anonymously before: what made you hit post? We're writing our onboarding and want to get this right."

### 1 viral loop upgrade: "Send it to one person"
- **Mechanic:** after a user's first post, offer "Send this to one person who'd get it" using the existing WhatsApp send and the share card. The sender gets 30 coins when that friend finishes onboarding (reward already exists).
- **Copy:** "Someone out there needs to read this. Send it. They won't know it was you unless you say."
- **Abuse risks:** self-referral farming (cap rewards per device/week), spammy sharing (limit sends per day), WhatsApp country prefix bug must be fixed first. Product-manager to decide cap.

### 1 offline stunt: "Say what you can't say" QR stickers
- Small stickers with a QR code and the line "Say what you can't say out loud. 18+." placed only where permitted (noticeboards that allow flyers, not private property).
- QR links to `/join?ref=sticker1` so scans are countable.
- Cost: roughly $15-40 for 200-500 stickers (rough estimate, vendor-dependent; unverified).
