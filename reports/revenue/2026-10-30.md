## What's working and what isn't
- Unknown from data. From the code: the core loop is sound (free Knock/Room visibility, pay only on accepted link up) and aligned with the "reward visibility and control" rule.
- Weakness: small ticket. Two packs of $0.99 and $1.99 cap spend per purchase, and each card transaction loses a fixed fee share (Stripe's fee structure is a fixed amount plus a percentage; check the current rate), which bites hardest at $0.49 tier 2.

## Experiments
1. **Bigger coin bundle.** Hypothesis: a third pack (for example 15 coins at about 20% off per coin) raises average purchase value without hurting conversion on the small packs. Change: add `bundle` to `_PACKAGES_BASE`, `STRIPE_PRICES`, and `COIN_PACKAGES`. Metric: average revenue per paying user, share of purchases that are the bundle. Risk: cannibalising Popular; keep the discount modest and test on a share of traffic.
2. **Boosts instead of paywalls.** Hypothesis: users who posted a hookup drop will pay coins to extend its live window (currently 12h, max 48h) or pin it higher. Change: a coin-priced "extend 12h" action using the existing `HOOKUP_MAX_LIVE_HOURS`. Metric: extend rate per hookup post, coins spent per poster. Risk: low; it is visibility, not the core loop, and the link up fee stays fixed.
3. **Premium trial framing.** Hypothesis: showing "premium makes link ups free" at the moment a user is about to pay their second link up converts more than a static premium page. Change: contextual prompt on the coin-spend screen after the 2nd spend in 7 days. Metric: premium conversion from that prompt. Risk: nagging; cap at once per week.
