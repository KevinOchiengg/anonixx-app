## Experiments
1. **Third coin bundle (carried over, urgent).** Hypothesis: a 30 or 50 coin pack (about 20% off per coin vs Popular) lets one purchase cover a Boost or paid drop and raises average order value. Change: add a bundle to `coins.py` and `geo_pricing.py`. Metric: revenue per paying user, bundle share, Boost and paid-drop conversion. Risk: cannibalises Popular; keep the discount modest.
2. **Boost prompt at the right moment.** Hypothesis: showing "Boost for 9 coins" when a hookup post gets its first Knock, or has none after 3 hours, converts better than the My posts entry alone. Metric: boost rate per hookup post. Risk: low; do not suggest it if the post is nearly expired.
3. **Refund-rate watch plus "poster replied" nudge.** Hypothesis: a push to posters with an unanswered paid Knock before the 24h refund lowers the refund rate and lifts retained link up revenue. Metric: refund rate, link up reply rate. Risk: low; do not pressure posters to accept.
