## Pricing and code changes since 2026-11-20
Read from code and commit titles; I did not review full diffs.
- **Link up refund window changed.** Last week's report said paid Knocks auto-refund after 24h (`REFUND_AFTER_HOURS = 24`). The code now has `REFUND_AFTER = timedelta(days=7)` in `unlock_requests.py` (same as a hookup's lifetime), with "Link Up refunded: no reply in 7 days". Effect: coins are held longer before a silent Knock is refunded, so fewer refunds hit reported revenue early, but buyers wait longer for their coins back. Watch refund rate and support complaints.
- **Charge-on-accept restored for commenter profiles** (`e5fc45e`): unlocking from a commenter's profile now sends a request the poster accepts or declines, and coins move only on accept. An earlier commit (`a1a3763`) unlocked straight away; it was replaced within the week. So there are two paths: one-tap paid Knock (refund after 7 days if silent) and request-then-accept. Founder: confirm which is intended and update the TEAM.md wording ("charged only if the poster accepts") if the pay-first path stays.
- **Boost is now super-admin only for pin and boost of official drops** (`f57a507`). This is admin tooling and does not change the user Boost price: `BOOST_COST = 9` coins, everyone pays.
- **Unchanged:** `DROP_POST_COST = 3` (hookup only; confessions and admin drops are free), `COINS_UNLOCK_COST = 6`, premium unlocks free (`PREMIUM_UNLOCK_COST = 0`), `ORIGIN_AUTHOR_UNLOCK_COST = 3`, paid video drops at 20/50/100/200 coins, packs Starter 3 coins and Popular 6 coins (`coins.py`; KES 49 and 99 for M-Pesa users, regional only), premium 1 month $9.99.

## Experiments
1. **Third coin bundle (carried over, urgent).** Hypothesis: a 30 or 50 coin pack (about 20% off per coin vs Popular) lets one purchase cover a Boost or paid drop and raises average order value. Change: add a bundle in `coins.py` and `geo_pricing.py`. Metric: revenue per paying user, bundle share, Boost and paid-drop conversion. Risk: cannibalises Popular; keep the discount modest.
2. **Boost prompt at the right moment.** Hypothesis: offering "Boost for 9 coins" after a hookup post's first Knock, or after 3 hours with none, beats the My posts entry alone. Metric: boost rate per hookup post. Risk: low; skip if the post is nearly expired.
3. **Silent-Knock nudge (updated for the 7-day window).** Hypothesis: a push to posters with an unanswered paid Knock at day 2 and day 5 raises reply rate and lowers refunds. Metric: refund rate, link up reply rate, support tickets about held coins. Risk: low; never pressure posters to accept.
